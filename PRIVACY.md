# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: February 15, 2026**

## Overview

BLITE is an open-source, end-to-end encrypted communication platform. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Data We Collect

### Encrypted Data (Cannot Be Read by BLITE)
- **Message content**: All direct message content is end-to-end encrypted using the Signal Protocol. BLITE servers cannot decrypt this content.
- **Voice/Video content**: Media streams are encrypted end-to-end. BLITE servers cannot access audio or video content.
- **Group/Channel messages**: Encrypted with symmetric sender keys distributed via E2E encrypted channels.

### Unencrypted Data (Metadata & Operational Data)
- **Account Information**: Email address, username, display name, optional phone number
- **Profile Data**: Avatar, banner, bio, custom status
- **Communication Metadata**: Who you communicate with, when, message timestamps, read receipts
- **Server Membership**: Which servers and channels you join, roles assigned
- **Social Graph**: Friend connections, friend requests, blocks
- **Interaction Data**: Reactions, pins, typing indicators
- **Technical Data**: IP addresses, connection logs, device identifiers (for push notifications)
- **Moderation Data**: Bans, kicks, audit logs of server actions

## How We Use Your Data

- **Service Operation**: To deliver messages, enable voice/video, manage servers
- **Authentication**: To verify your identity and maintain sessions
- **Moderation**: To enforce rules and handle abuse reports
- **Notifications**: To send push notifications for new messages
- **Analytics**: Basic operational metrics (server performance, error rates)

## Data Retention

- **Encrypted Messages**: Stored until manually deleted or until expiry settings activate
- **Metadata**: Retained indefinitely for operational purposes
- **Account Data**: Retained while your account is active
- **Logs**: Connection logs retained for 90 days

## Your Rights

### GDPR Rights (European Users)
- **Access**: Request a copy of your data
- **Deletion**: Request account deletion (metadata may be retained for legal compliance)
- **Portability**: Export your data
- **Correction**: Update incorrect information
- **Objection**: Object to certain data processing

### CCPA Rights (California Users)
- Right to know what data is collected
- Right to deletion
- Right to opt-out of data sales (BLITE does not sell data)

**To exercise your rights, contact**: support@blite.chat

## Data Sharing

BLITE does **not sell** your data. We may share data:
- **Legal Requirements**: We must comply with valid law enforcement requests and court orders. However, due to end-to-end encryption, we can only provide:
  - **Metadata**: Who communicated with whom, timestamps, IP addresses, server membership, account information
  - **Message Content**: Mathematically impossible to access - we do not have decryption keys
  - This is a fundamental limitation (and feature) of E2E encryption
- **Self-Hosted Instances**: If you use a self-hosted instance, the instance operator controls all data and legal compliance

## Security

- End-to-end encryption for message content
- Passwords hashed with bcrypt
- TLS/SSL for data in transit
- Regular security updates

However, no system is completely secure. Use strong passwords and enable recovery keys.

## Children's Privacy

BLITE is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us immediately.

## International Transfers

BLITE servers may be located in various jurisdictions. By using BLITE, you consent to international data transfers.

## Self-Hosting

BLITE is open-source and can be self-hosted. Self-hosted instances are controlled by the instance operator, not BLITE. This Privacy Policy applies only to the official BLITE instance.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
